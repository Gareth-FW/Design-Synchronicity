# AGENT.md

## Repository purpose

Design-Synchronicity is a writing and thinking repository about synchronistic design: the idea that an organisation's promise, product capability, service experience and human context must align at the moments that matter.

The repository's central position is that the experience is the organisation. When brand, product, interaction and accessibility remain coherent, people can act with clarity, trust and confidence.

## Primary documents

- `README.md` — project overview and repository framing.
- `The_Synchronicity_of_Good_Experience_Draft.md` — longer essay and conceptual argument.
- `Synchronistic_Design_Principles.md` — practical design framework and principles.
- `LICENSE` — MIT license.

## Core design philosophy

This repository is grounded in the following ideas:

- align organisational promise with real product and service capability;
- design around human intention rather than system process;
- respond at the moment of need;
- preserve orientation, trust and control across a journey;
- ensure equivalent experiences for different people, contexts and access needs;
- treat accessibility and inclusion as core design constraints, not after-the-fact checks.

## Guidance for agents

When working in this repository:

- treat content as conceptual and editorial rather than application code;
- keep writing human-centred, precise and reflective;
- preserve the repository's tone: thoughtful, practical, and grounded in experience design;
- favour language that emphasises alignment between organisation, product and person;
- avoid reducing the work to generic UX slogans or product-process language without context;
- keep emphasis on inclusion, dignity, agency and equivalent experience.

## Editing expectations

- Prefer small, well-scoped markdown updates over large rewrites.
- Maintain the repository's existing naming conventions and document structure.
- When adding or revising content, tie it back to the synchronistic design argument.
- If a new section is added, ensure it supports the core themes of alignment, timing, context and equivalence.
- Use inclusive, non-technical language where possible unless a specific technical or operational distinction is necessary.

## Quality bar

A contribution is high quality when it:

- reads clearly and professionally;
- reinforces the repository's central thesis;
- speaks to the relationship between organisational promise and lived experience;
- considers accessibility, inclusion and different human conditions as core to design quality;
- avoids contradictory framing that separates brand, product and experience.
